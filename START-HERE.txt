FOOTBALL TRACKER - START HERE
=============================

What this is: one project that is BOTH a website and a phone app.
Open the address on any phone or computer. On a phone you can add it to
your home screen so it looks and opens like a normal app.

It starts in DEMO MODE (made-up matches) so you can see it working with
no sign-ups. Add your data key to switch to real matches.

--- WHAT YOU NEED ---
1. A free API-Football key (this is your real data source)
     Sign up at: https://www.api-football.com
     Copy the "API key" from your account page.
2. A free GitHub account (a place to keep these files): https://github.com
3. A free Render account (puts the app on the internet): https://render.com

--- PUT IT ONLINE (about 20 minutes, easiest on a computer) ---
1. Unzip this folder on your computer.
2. On GitHub: click "New repository", name it football-tracker,
   then "uploading an existing file", drag in ALL the files and the
   "public" folder from this project, and click "Commit changes".
3. On Render: click New > Web Service > connect your GitHub >
   choose football-tracker.
4. Fill in:
     Build Command:  npm install
     Start Command:  npm start
     Instance type:  Free
5. Under "Environment Variables" add:
     API_KEY  =  (paste your API-Football key)
   Leave it out to stay in demo mode.
6. Click "Create Web Service". Wait a few minutes.
7. Render shows your address (like https://football-tracker.onrender.com).
   Open it on your phone.

--- INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE ---
iPhone (use Safari):  Share button > "Add to Home Screen"
Android (use Chrome): Menu (3 dots) > "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"

--- GOOD TO KNOW ---
- The free Render plan goes to sleep when nobody uses it. The first
  open after a break can take about a minute.
- The free API-Football plan allows 100 requests a day. That is enough
  to test, but live scores update roughly every 5 minutes and can run
  out on a busy day. A paid plan fixes this: then add an environment
  variable LIVE_POLL_SECONDS = 30 and DAILY_LIMIT = (your plan's number).
- News (optional): add NEWS_FEEDS = link to a news site's RSS feed
  (separate several with commas). The app only shows the headline, the
  source name and a link back to the original. Check each site's terms
  first. Many allow personal, non-commercial use only.

--- TRY IT ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER FIRST (optional) ---
Install Node.js (version 20 or newer) from https://nodejs.org, then in
this folder run:   npm install    then    npm start
Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.
